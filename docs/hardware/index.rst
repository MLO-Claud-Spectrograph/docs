Instrument hardware
===================

This section records the principal optical, mechanical, and detector components
in the spectrograph. Vendor specifications describe the purchased parts; the
instrument model and as-built measurements remain authoritative for quantities
that depend on alignment, spacing, focus, or a particular production unit.

The optical path inside the instrument can be summarized as follows: A diverging
beam leaves the fiber, is collimated, passes through the order-blocking filter
(if installed), is dispersed by the ruled reflection grating, and is imaged by
the camera lens onto the detector. The grating mount sets the grating
orientation, and the Pinefeat controller provides electronic focus and aperture
control for the camera lens.
