Grating mount: Newport DGA-50
=============================

The ``DGA-50`` is a three-axis adjustable grating mount sized for 50 mm-high
square or rectangular optics. It provides pitch, yaw, and roll adjustment, with
lockable 100-TPI adjusters. The mounting geometry places the front surface of a
6 mm-thick optic on the post rotation axis.

.. list-table:: Key catalog properties
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 40 60

   * - Property
     - Value
   * - Adjustments
     - Pitch, yaw, and roll
   * - Angular range
     - ±2°
   * - Adjustment threads
     - 100 TPI, lockable
   * - Nominal optic thickness
     - 6 mm
   * - Optical-axis height
     - 38.1 mm
   * - Material
     - Anodized aluminum

The mount's fine adjustment is intended for alignment and focus optimization;
the nominal 52° instrument axis separation is set by the surrounding mechanical
layout rather than by the mount's ±2° adjustment range.
